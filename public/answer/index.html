Before you consider it

Honest answers to the usual concerns.

Will it make up a price or appointment?

It should not. Pricing boundaries are configured, and an appointment is described as confirmed only after the booking tool returns confirmation.

Does this replace every human conversation?

No. Human requests, sensitive situations, unclear service-area cases, and defined exceptions need an intentional transfer or follow-up path.

What if I am not ready to move all call handling?

The full receptionist remains the main design. If a specific coverage window is the only acceptable starting point, the Pest Backup Line can be considered after the gap and success criteria are defined.

Are customer results being claimed here?

No. Elixis is not presenting pest-control customer results, savings, booking lifts, or uptime statistics. Fit should be judged through the configured demo, scenario tests, and transparent rollout limits.

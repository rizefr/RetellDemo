The calls are specific

A useful receptionist notices the difference.

Each call type needs its own questions, boundaries, and handoff—not one “How may I help?” branch with a calendar at the end.

NEW LEAD

“I found ants in the kitchen.”

Capture the issue, basic property context, service location, callback number, and desired next step without diagnosing the pest over the phone.

  • Ask practical intake questions
  • Avoid unsupported treatment or price claims
  • Move toward the configured booking or follow-up path
EXISTING CUSTOMER

“I still see activity after service.”

Recognize that this is not a new-sale script. Capture the account context the team needs and route according to the business’s follow-up policy.

  • Do not promise a service outcome
  • Do not invent warranty terms
  • Keep the caller from repeating the full story
URGENT OR HUMAN

“I need to speak with someone.”

Use the defined urgent and human-request path. Automation should make exceptions more visible, not trap the caller inside a script.

  • Recognize the request
  • Follow approved transfer or callback rules
  • Preserve context for the person who receives it

Fit and rollout

Questions that should be resolved before launch.

Can it follow our actual service-area rules?

The configuration can use the service-area information and tools the business provides. When coverage is uncertain, the safe path is to capture the request and let the team confirm rather than reject or promise service.

Can it book over the phone?

The receptionist can be configured around a connected booking flow. It should confirm an appointment only after the booking tool succeeds; otherwise it records the request and sets an honest follow-up expectation.

How does the Backup Line fit?

The full receptionist is the primary offer. A Pest Backup Line is a lower-risk entry only when the owner identifies a specific window—such as overflow or after-hours—and wants limited scope before considering expansion.

Does “24/7” guarantee uninterrupted uptime?

No uptime percentage is claimed. “Elixis Pest Receptionist 24/7” is the product name and intended coverage model; actual reliability depends on the configured telephony, integrations, monitoring, and fallback plan.
